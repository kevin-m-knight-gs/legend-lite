## How the diagnoses were checked

1. **Citation resolution** — all 2,162 `file:line` citations resolve to a real file
   with that line (100%); 92.3% content-confirmed by keyword match within ±8 lines.
2. **Adversarial refutation** — the 31 cheap high-confidence real defects were handed
   to verifiers told to refute them: 23 CONFIRMED, 8 PARTIALLY_WRONG, **0 REFUTED**,
   and **15 of 31 effort estimates revised upward**. Entries carrying an
   `adversarial review` row have been pressure-tested; the rest have not, and their
   XS/S estimates should be treated as provisional.
3. **Completeness reconciliation** — every fan-out output was checked test-by-test
   against its input list. One agent returned a well-formed result covering 8 of 36
   tests; it was re-run split.

## Regenerating

The diagnoses are model output, not a deterministic derivation — they cannot be
regenerated byte-identically. What *can* be re-derived cheaply is whether they are
still current: parse the `### per-test outcomes (non-passing)` section of
`docs/RELATIONAL_CORPUS.md` and diff the `(family, test)` keys against
`diagnoses.csv`. Anything present there and absent here is new since this pass.
